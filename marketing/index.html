The flow

How a trade works, start to finish.

Seven simple steps — browsing is open to everyone, trading unlocks once you’re verified.

Sign up

Create a free account with email. Browse every listing right away.

Get verified

Upload a selfie + government ID. Our team approves you — now you can sell, message and make offers.

List or browse

Post an item with photos, or search & filter by category, price and condition.

Make an offer & negotiate

Send an offer. The seller can accept, decline or counter — back and forth until you agree.

The buyer pays into a protected hold. The money is not sent straight to the seller.

The seller ships. Both sides follow the order status: paid → shipped → delivered.

Confirm & release

Buyer confirms the item arrived as described — the payment is released to the seller.

Safe payments

The money is protected until you get your item.

Instead of sending cash directly to a stranger, the payment is held in the middle and only released when the buyer confirms delivery. If something goes wrong, it goes to dispute — not to the scammer.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to verify to browse?

No — anyone can browse and search every listing for free. Verification is only required to sell, message a seller, or make an offer.

How does verification work?

You upload a selfie and a photo of a government ID (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, etc.). Our team reviews it and approves or rejects with a reason. Your ID number is masked everywhere except the reviewer’s screen.

How is my payment protected?

When you buy, your payment is held securely rather than sent straight to the seller. It’s only released once you confirm the item arrived as described. If there’s a problem, you can open a dispute before release.

What can I trade?

Sneakers, streetwear, collectibles, apparel, accessories and more. Every listing goes through the same verified, protected process.

How does TradeX make money?

A small commission is taken from each completed, released order — so we only earn when a trade actually goes through safely.
